# Fact sheet

- OpenAI released GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna on 9 July 2026. Sol is priced at $5 per million input tokens and $30 per million output tokens. (Artificial Analysis on X, 9 July 2026, 17:20 UTC, https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2075268970492657905)
- Claude Fable 5, launched in June 2026, is priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. (Finout, launch-day pricing post, datePublished 10 June 2026, https://www.finout.io/blog/claude-fable-5-mythos-5-pricing-benchmarks)
- Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: "GPT-5.6 Sol (max) scores 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (max) in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59 points, at approximately one third of the cost." Sol costs $1.04 per Intelligence Index task. (Artificial Analysis, 9 July 2026, same URL)
- Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index: Sol (max) in Codex leads all three evaluations, and "its per task cost is ~40% and ~10% cheaper than Claude Fable 5 (max) and Opus 4.8 (max) respectively in Claude Code." (Artificial Analysis, 9 July 2026, same URL)
- Token use: Sol (max) uses about 15k output tokens per Intelligence Index task; Artificial Analysis says it "uses fewer output tokens than most models of comparable intelligence" and "defines a new Pareto frontier of Intelligence vs Output Tokens per Task". Artificial Analysis gives no Fable 5 token figure in this thread. (Artificial Analysis, 9 July 2026, same URL, and https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2075268984539410521)
- On AA-Briefcase (knowledge work), Fable 5 (max) leads and Sol (max) ranks second; Fable's rubric score is 56% against 42% for Sol. (Artificial Analysis, 9 July 2026, same URL)
- ARC Prize verified Sol on ARC-AGI-2 at 92% for $1.44 per task and on ARC-AGI-1 at 96.5% for $0.54 per task. ARC Prize had published no Fable 5 result at the time. (ARC Prize on X, 9 July 2026, 17:28 UTC, https://x.com/arcprize/status/2075270883271454984)
