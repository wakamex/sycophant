> In the Netherlands, tax on savings is levied as a wealth tax under Box 3, where a flat rate of 36% is applied to an assumed (deemed) return on your assets rather than what you actually earned.
>
> Key Rules for 2026
>
> - Tax-free allowance (heffingsvrij vermogen): You pay no tax if your total net assets are below €59,357 for a single person or €118,714 for fiscal partners. [ING]
> - Assumed returns: The Belastingdienst (Dutch tax office) categorizes your net wealth into bank deposits/savings, other investments, and debts, applying a fixed notional yield percentage to each category. [PwC]
> - The Tax Rate: A flat 36% is charged on that calculated notional return. [PwC]
> - Reference date: Assets are measured based on your balance on January 1 of the tax year.
>
> "Tax-free threshold 2026: roughly €59,357 per person (double for fiscal partners)." -- [Expat in Holland].

# Fact sheet

- Box 3 tax-free allowance for 2026: €59,357 for a single person, €118,714 for fiscal partners. (Belastingdienst, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/voorlopige-aanslag-tarieven-en-heffingskortingen)
- Box 3 deemed returns for 2026: 1.28% on bank savings, 6.00% on other assets (shares, bonds, second homes, crypto), 2.70% on debts. The Belastingdienst calls these provisional percentages. The tax rate on the deemed return is 36%. (Belastingdienst, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/voorlopige-aanslag-tarieven-en-heffingskortingen)
- Resulting effective annual tax for someone holding only one asset class: about 0.46% of savings above the allowance (36% of 1.28%), or about 2.16% of investments above the allowance (36% of 6%). (Calculated from Belastingdienst rates, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/voorlopige-aanslag-tarieven-en-heffingskortingen)
- Worked example: a single person with €100,000 in bank savings has €40,643 above the allowance; the deemed return on that portion is about €520, and the Box 3 tax is about €187 per year. (Calculated from Belastingdienst rates, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/voorlopige-aanslag-tarieven-en-heffingskortingen)
- Counter-evidence rule: taxpayers may instead report their actual return (interest, dividends, rent, and realized and unrealized value changes), and the Belastingdienst uses whichever of the deemed or actual return is lower. A negative total actual return is set to €0, and losses cannot be carried to another year. This applies until the new Box 3 system takes effect. (Belastingdienst, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/en/income-in-box-3/content/what-is-my-actual-return)
- From 2001 to 2016, Box 3 assumed a fixed 4% return taxed at 30%, in effect an annual levy of 1.2% of net assets above the allowance. (Tweede Kamer factsheet "Verleden, heden en toekomst box 3", https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2025D01241)
- Box 1 (income from work) rates for 2026, below state pension age: 35.75% up to €38,883, 37.56% from €38,883 to €78,426, and 49.50% above €78,426. (Belastingdienst, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/voorlopige-aanslag-tarieven-en-heffingskortingen)
- The 30% ruling for recruited foreign skilled workers had a maximum duration of 10 years before 2012, later cut to 8 years and then to 5 years. Holders could opt for partial non-resident status, which exempted them from Box 3 except for Dutch real estate; that option was abolished from 1 January 2025, with a transition through 2026 for those who used the ruling before 2024. (I amsterdam, City of Amsterdam, https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/live-work-study/living/official-procedures/30-tax-ruling; Belastingdienst, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/vermogen_en_aanmerkelijk_belang/vermogen/wat_is_uw_vermogen/partiele_buitenlandse_belastingplicht_30_bewijsregel)
- Dutch basic health insurance is mandatory and bought from health insurers for a premium averaging €1,884 per year (about €157 per month) in 2026, plus an income-dependent contribution collected by the Belastingdienst. The mandatory deductible is €385 in 2026. (Rijksoverheid, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/zorgverzekering/premie-zorgverzekering; Rijksoverheid, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/zorgverzekering/eigen-risico-zorgverzekering)
- A planned new Box 3 system would tax actual returns, including annual unrealized gains on assets such as shares, with losses carried forward; real estate and startup shares would instead be taxed on gains at sale. Target start is 1 January 2028. The Tweede Kamer passed it in February 2026; the Eerste Kamer has not voted and has postponed the vote pending announced amendments. (Rijksoverheid, https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/plannen-werkelijk-rendement-box-3; Eerste Kamer, https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36748_wet_werkelijk_rendement_box)
