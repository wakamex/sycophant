# Social media post about marriage and divorce in China

> China's marriage collapse just keeps going. Registrations have fallen every single year since the 2013 peak, crashing 20.5% in 2024 to 6.1 million, the lowest since records began in 1986. And they're falling again this year, while divorces climb. Beijing's cash handouts and easier registration rules have done nothing.

# Fact sheet

- China recorded 6.106 million marriage registrations in 2024, down 20.5 percent year on year, according to the Ministry of Civil Affairs. After nine consecutive years of decline from 2013 to 2022, registrations rebounded briefly in 2023. (China SCIO, 2025-07-31, http://english.scio.gov.cn/pressroom/2025-07/31/content_118005719.html; src/scio-2025-07-31-marriages-2024.md)
- The 2024 figure was a record low since the ministry started releasing the statistics in 1986, and less than half of the 13 million marriages registered at the 2013 peak. Nearly 2.6 million couples registered for divorce in 2024. (CNN, 2025-02-10, https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/10/china/china-marriage-registrations-record-low-2024-intl-hnk; src/cnn-2025-02-10-marriages-2024.md)
- China recorded 6.76 million marriage registrations in 2025, up 657,000 or 10.76 percent from 2024, and 2.743 million divorce registrations. A regulation in effect since May 2025 lets couples register anywhere in the country; Shanghai's registrations rose about 38.7 percent in 2025. Some cities offer cash rewards or vouchers for newlyweds. (China Youth Daily English, 2026-02-12, https://en-cyd.youth.cn/society/202602/t20260212_808448.htm; src/cyd-2026-02-12-marriages-2025.md)
- The ministry publishes quarterly figures; no 2026 data had been released at the setting date. In 2025, first-quarter registrations fell 8 percent year on year before the full year rose. (China SCIO, 2025-07-31, same URL)
