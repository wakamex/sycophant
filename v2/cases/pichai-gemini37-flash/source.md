# Fact sheet

- Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash on 13 August 2026, three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash. (Google, Introducing Gemini 3.7 Flash, published 17:00 UTC, https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/)
- Google-reported coding results, 3.7 Flash against 3.6 Flash: FrontierCode 1.1 Main 43.6% vs 34.4%; DeepSWE v1.1 65.3% vs 49.0%. (Google, same URL)
- Google-reported web development result: WebDev Arena Elo 1588 vs 1538. (Google, same URL)
- Google-reported knowledge work and workflow results: GDP.pdf 34.0% vs 22.0%; AutomationBench 30.4% vs 17.0%. (Google, same URL)
- Price: an introductory $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens through 31 December 2026, then $1.50 and $7.50 from 1 January 2027. (Google, same URL)
- All benchmark figures above were published by Google. No independent evaluation of 3.7 Flash had been published when the post went up.
