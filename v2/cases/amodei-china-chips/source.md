> To address these concerns, I do support the following three measures, which I and Anthropic have consistently advocated for:
>
> We should not sell powerful chips or chipmaking equipment to China, and we should crack down on the rampant smuggling and workarounds used to obtain access to such chips. China has limited domestic production capacity, and therefore, due to the scaling laws, cannot build more powerful models than the US without US chips. This is the most efficient and direct way to block threat #1, and by hampering the training of models that are out of reach of US law, it also indirectly helps with threat #2.

- In March 2026 the Justice Department unsealed an indictment charging three people with conspiring to divert AI servers to China; the FBI said they 'conspired to sell billions of dollars' worth of servers integrating sensitive, controlled graphic processing units to buyers in China'. (US Department of Justice, 19 March 2026, https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/three-charged-conspiring-unlawfully-divert-cutting-edge-us-artificial-intelligence; this is the smuggling case the post links to)

- Epoch AI gave Kimi K3 an Epoch Capabilities Index score of 156, "between Opus 4.6, and GPT 5.4, which released in February and March 2026 respectively". (Epoch AI on X, 21 July 2026, https://x.com/EpochAIResearch/status/2079602012644360382)
- A transcript said to be from a 20 May 2026 talk by DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng circulated on 23 July 2026; its authenticity has not been verified. In it Liang says DeepSeek has "about 20,000 H-equivalent compute cards", works "with one-twentieth of the U.S. compute", and that "Huawei 950 'super nodes' can substitute Nvidia GB200/GB300 in performance and price. In the same transcript Liang says domestic chips' 'only problem is insufficient production capacity' and that Huawei's problem 'is still insufficient production capacity'. (Geopolitechs translation, 23 July 2026, https://www.geopolitechs.org/p/deepseek-founder-liang-wenfeng-in, which summarizes: "The ecosystem is fine; capacity is the bottleneck."; Fred Gao translation, 23 July 2026, https://www.fredgao.com/p/deepseeks-liang-wenfeng-breaks-his, which notes "There are no details available to verify the authenticity of the talk")
