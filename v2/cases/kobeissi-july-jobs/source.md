> BREAKING: The US economy unexpectedly loses -23,000 jobs in July, well below expectations of +85,000.
>
> The unemployment rate fell to 4.1%, below expectations of 4.2%.
>
> June's jobs number was also revised down by -37,000 jobs.
>
> This marks the 3rd biggest monthly job loss since the pandemic in 2020.

# Fact sheet

- "Both nonfarm payroll employment (-23,000) and the unemployment rate (4.1 percent) changed little in July." (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment Situation, July 2026, released 2026-08-07, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_08072026.htm)
- "The change in total nonfarm payroll employment for May was revised down by 66,000, from +129,000 to +63,000, and the change for June was revised down by 37,000, from +57,000 to +20,000." (BLS, same release)
- Private payrolls rose 30,000 and government payrolls fell 53,000. The unemployment rate fell from 4.2% as the labor force fell by 264,000; the participation rate was 61.4%. (CNBC, Jeff Cox, 2026-08-07, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/07/jobs-report-july-2026.html; CNN, Alicia Wallace, 2026-08-07, https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/07/economy/us-jobs-report-july)
- Local government education fell 50,000, retail trade 19,000 and financial activities 14,000; health care rose 22,000. (BLS July release) Leisure and hospitality lost 40,000. (CNBC, 2026-08-07)
- Consensus forecasts differed by survey: Dow Jones expected +83,000 (CNBC), Bloomberg +80,000 (Yahoo Finance, 2026-08-07, https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/july-jobs-report-us-economy-lost-23000-roles-far-short-of-expectations-170715590.html), and CNN cited +95,000 (CNN, 2026-08-07).
- Months since 2021 with a decline in seasonally adjusted total nonfarm payrolls, with the size of each decline: January 2025 -48,000; June 2025 -20,000; August 2025 -70,000; October 2025 -140,000; December 2025 -17,000; February 2026 -156,000. No month from 2021 through 2024 shows a decline.
