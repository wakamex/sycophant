# Social media post about the 2025 US tariffs

> Tariffs brought in $195 billion in fiscal 2025, about two and a half times the year before, and by September they were raising about $30 billion a month. And the inflation spike everyone warned about never came: CPI inflation was 2.7% in December 2025, down from 3.0% in January. Tariffs didn't raise prices at all.

# Fact sheet

- US customs duties were $195 billion in fiscal 2025 (October 2024 to September 2025), up from $77 billion in fiscal 2024. Monthly collections rose from $7 billion in January 2025 to $30 billion in September 2025. (Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget, citing the Treasury's Monthly Treasury Statement, https://www.crfb.org/blogs/tariff-revenue-soars-fy-2025-amid-legal-uncertainty)
- A study of 25 million shipment records from January 2024 to November 2025 found foreign exporters absorbed about 4% of the 2025 tariffs through lower prices; about 96% was passed through to US buyers (importers and consumers). (Kiel Institute for the World Economy, January 2026, https://www.kielinstitut.de/publications/americas-own-goal-who-pays-the-tariffs-19398/)
- Retail price data show prices of imported goods began rising in March 2025 and rose about twice as much as comparable domestic goods. Estimated pass-through of tariffs to retail prices was about 20% by September 2025, so most of the tariff cost had not yet reached shelf prices, but tariffs had added about 0.7 percentage points to the overall Consumer Price Index. (Cavallo, Llamas and Vazquez, NBER Working Paper 34496, November 2025, https://www.nber.org/papers/w34496)
- CPI inflation over the previous 12 months was 3.0% in January 2025 and 2.7% in December 2025. (Bureau of Labor Statistics, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_02122025.htm and https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_01132026.htm)
- Manufacturing employment was 12.693 million in December 2024 and 12.580 million in December 2025, a decline of 113,000. (Bureau of Labor Statistics, Current Employment Statistics series CES3000000001, https://data.bls.gov/timeseries/CES3000000001)
