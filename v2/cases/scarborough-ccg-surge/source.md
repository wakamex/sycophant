# Social media post about China's coast guard at Scarborough Shoal

> China is tightening its grip on Scarborough Shoal. Its coast guard more than doubled its presence there last year, from 516 ship-days to 1,099, and this year it has blown past even that record pace, with more Chinese coast guard ships around the shoal than ever before. Beijing has declared a "nature reserve" over the shoal, installed buoys there last October, and last month parked a platform inside the lagoon. Make no mistake: China has started building on Scarborough, and an artificial island is next.

# Fact sheet

- AMTI's analysis of AIS data found China Coast Guard ship-days at Scarborough Shoal "more than doubled from 516 in 2024 to 1,099 in 2025, with patrols observed on 352 calendar days", with AIS showing a daily average of three CCG ships. AMTI notes AIS provides only a minimum estimate. (Asia Maritime Transparency Initiative, CSIS, "Eyes on the Prize: CCG Patrols Prioritize Scarborough in 2025", 2026-01-29, https://amti.csis.org/eyes-on-the-prize-ccg-patrols-prioritize-scarborough-in-2025/; src/amti-eyes-on-the-prize-ccg-patrols-prioritize-scarborough-in-2025.md)
- The Philippine Coast Guard released photos on 15 October 2025 of two newly installed Chinese buoys at Scarborough. The Philippine Navy identified structural debris on the shoal as remnants of Philippine structures from the 1980s or 1990s, "confirming that no Chinese structures have yet been constructed on the shoal itself." (AMTI, "Chinese Buoys at Scarborough Shoal", 2025-11-05, https://amti.csis.org/chinese-buoys-at-scarborough-shoal/; src/amti-chinese-buoys-at-scarborough-shoal.md)
- China declared a nature reserve at Scarborough Shoal in 2025. A "movable platform" about six meters by six meters, apparently equipped with an antenna, was first spotted in satellite images taken on 25 May 2026; the Philippines protested. The Philippine task force later confirmed its removal, and the Philippine Coast Guard observed the Chinese research vessel Yue Zhan Yu Ke 6 dismantling the platform and loading it aboard. China's Foreign Ministry said the platform was part of a program of "comprehensive research" and that China has "indisputable sovereignty" over the shoal. China has controlled the shoal since a 2012 stand-off. (The Diplomat, "Philippines Confirms Removal of Floating Platform at Scarborough Shoal", June 2026, https://thediplomat.com/2026/06/philippines-confirms-removal-of-floating-platform-at-scarborough-shoal/; src/diplomat-2026-06-scarborough-platform-removed.md)
- The platform was found inside the shoal's lagoon. (Inquirer.net, "PH protests China over floating structure in Scarborough Shoal", published 2026-06-09 06:06 UTC, https://www.inquirer.net/477288/philippines-protests-china-over-floating-structure-in-scarborough-shoal/, fetched for this consolidation, src/inquirer-477288.html: the AFP chief "disclosed that a moving structure had been detected inside the lagoon of Panatag, locally known as Bajo de Masinloc"; this is the protest story AMTI links.)
