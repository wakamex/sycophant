# Fact sheet

- Xsignal data reported by Beike Caijing (新京报贝壳财经) on 29 July 2026: Qwen App (千问) monthly active users rose "从1月的0.72亿迅速升至2月的3.22亿" (from 72 million in January to 322 million in February) during a Spring Festival sign-up campaign, "活动结束后回落至1.7亿至1.9亿之间，6月重新突破2亿关口" (after the campaign fell back to between 170 and 190 million, and in June passed 200 million again). Doubao's June MAU "升至5.28亿，创下新高" (rose to a record 528 million). (Tencent News syndication, published 2026-07-29 11:39, https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260729A055F600)
- QuestMobile, H1 2026: "2026年6月，豆包、千问、DeepSeek活跃用户规模分别为3.82亿、1.67亿和1.30亿" (in June 2026 Doubao, Qwen and DeepSeek had 382 million, 167 million and 130 million active users). (First reported by Sina Tech, 2026-07-14, https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/digi/2026-07-14/doc-inihtvuk5352510.shtml; quoted text from QuestMobile's H1 report, Tencent News syndication, 2026-08-04, https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260804A06CLJ00)
- On 7 August 2026 the Qwen App and PC client added several features, "推出思考研究、定时任务、办公助理、智能体广场、语音通话等多项新功能" (a "thinking research" mode, scheduled tasks, an office assistant, an agent plaza and voice calls). "上述功能所有用户均可免费使用" (all users can use these features free), and "面向专业用户，办公助理还将支持付费获取更多使用额度" (for professional users, the office assistant will also offer paid extra usage quota). (Sina Tech, 2026-08-07 10:16 Beijing time, https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/shenji/2026-08-07/doc-inimnenr0035717.shtml)
- On the same call, CFO Toby Xu said of the AI Labs and Applications segment: "Its adjusted EBITDA was a loss of RMB 13.9 billion, primarily due to our increased investment in AI capabilities and higher inference costs related to Qwen app. The loss significantly narrowed quarter-over-quarter due to the reduction in marketing expenses for Qwen app." (Motley Fool transcript; the transcript renders adjusted EBITA as "EBITDA")
