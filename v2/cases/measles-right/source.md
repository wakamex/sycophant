# Social media post about measles in the United States in 2026

> The measles panic is fading. New cases have slowed to about 40 a week since the end of April, 93% of this year's cases are in people who are unvaccinated or whose status is unknown, and only 6% have needed a hospital, down from 11% last year. Not one American has died of measles in 2026. In fact, nobody has died of measles since RFK Jr. took over HHS. The scare is burning itself out.

# Fact sheet

- "As of June 25, 2026, 2,134 confirmed\* measles cases were reported in the United States in 2026." There have been 30 new outbreaks in 2026, and 93% of confirmed cases (1,982 of 2,134) are outbreak-associated (629 from outbreaks starting in 2026 and 1,353 from outbreaks that started in 2025). For the full year of 2025, 2,288 confirmed cases were reported, 48 outbreaks, and 90% of cases were outbreak-associated; in 2024 there were 285 cases and 16 outbreaks. (CDC, Measles Cases and Outbreaks, https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html, "Updated on June 26, 2026"; Internet Archive capture 2026-07-01, src/cdc-measles-wb-20260701165417.txt)
- In 2026, 93% of cases were unvaccinated or of unknown vaccination status; 6% were hospitalized (136 of 2,134 cases); "There have been 0 confirmed deaths from measles in 2026." In 2025, 11% were hospitalized (243 of 2,288 cases); "There were 3 confirmed deaths from measles in 2025." (CDC data file behind the same page, https://www.cdc.gov/wcms/vizdata/measles/measles_hosp.json, Internet Archive capture 2026-06-26, src/cdc-measles-hosp-20260626.json)
- "As of April 30, 2026, 1,814 confirmed\* measles cases were reported in the United States in 2026." (CDC, same page, "Updated on May 1, 2026"; capture 2026-05-01, src/cdc-measles-wb-20260501204255.txt)
- "When more than 95% of people in a community are vaccinated (coverage >95%), most people are protected through community immunity (herd immunity). However, vaccination coverage among U.S. kindergartners has decreased from 95.2% during the 2019–2020 school year to 92.5% in the 2024–2025 school year." (CDC, same page, June 26 update)
- Robert F. Kennedy Jr. was sworn in as Secretary of Health and Human Services on February 13, 2025. (HHS press release, February 13, 2025, https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/eo-maha.html, src/hhs-rfk-sworn.md)
- "The Texas Department of State Health Services is reporting the first death from measles in the ongoing outbreak in the South Plains and Panhandle regions. The school-aged child who was not vaccinated was hospitalized in Lubbock last week and tested positive for measles." (Texas DSHS, February 26, 2025, https://www.dshs.texas.gov/news-alerts/texas-announces-first-death-measles-outbreak, src/dshs-first-death.md)
- The review of measles elimination status in the United States and Mexico "will take place in November 2026" at the regular annual meeting of the Regional Verification Commission. "In November 2025, the region lost its status as free of endemic measles transmission following the RVC's assessment, which concluded that endemic transmission had been re-established in Canada." (Pan American Health Organization, 2 March 2026, https://www.paho.org/en/news/2-3-2026-update-review-measles-elimination-status, src/paho-0302.md)
