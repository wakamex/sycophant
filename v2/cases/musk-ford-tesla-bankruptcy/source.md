> Tesla & Ford are the only American carmakers not to have gone bankrupt out of 1000’s of car startups. Prototypes are easy, production is hard & being cash flow positive is excruciating.

# Fact sheet

- Chrysler filed for bankruptcy on April 30, 2009, and General Motors on June 1, 2009. (ClickOnDetroit, https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/2019/07/29/10-years-later-a-look-back-on-the-automotive-bankruptcy/)
- Ford did not file for bankruptcy and did not take the emergency federal loans that GM and Chrysler received. It had mortgaged all its assets for $23.6 billion in private loans in 2006. (The New York Times via CNBC, https://www.cnbc.com/2009/04/09/how-ford-avoided-the-meltdown-that-hit-gm-chrysler.html)
- Tesla has never filed for bankruptcy. Musk has said its December 2008 funding round closed when Tesla was 3 days away from bankruptcy. (Elon Musk on X, https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1323637665829396480)
- Studebaker, once a major US automaker, went into receivership in March 1933. (The Henry Ford, https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/articles/remembering-studebakers-bankruptcy-march-18-1933)
- American Motors Corporation (AMC) ended as an independent carmaker when it agreed in May 1987 to be acquired by Chrysler, not through bankruptcy. (Automotive History, https://automotivehistory.org/chrysler-buys-amc/)
- Rivian and Lucid, US electric carmakers, were operating and had not gone bankrupt when the post was made. Rivian began customer deliveries in September 2021 and Lucid in late October 2021. (Rivian prospectus, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000119312521328239/d157488d424b4.htm; Lucid Group, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1811210/000110465921120301/tm2128669d1_ex99-1.htm)
- Many US car startups did go bankrupt. For example, Fisker Automotive filed for bankruptcy in November 2013. (Bloomberg, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2013-11-22/fisker-files-for-bankruptcy-with-plan-to-sell-assets)
