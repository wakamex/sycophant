# Fact sheet

- On Thursday 30 July the yen surged in a move analysts said "bore the hallmarks of official intervention" by Tokyo; the dollar fell by as much as 3% to 158.34 yen, "down from 40-year highs hit earlier this week". The finance ministry could not be reached for comment, and "It was not immediately clear what drove the market move or whether Japanese authorities were in the market." (CNBC with Reuters, "Yen surges, analysts suspect official Japanese intervention", published 2026-07-30 01:27 UTC, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/dollar-steadies-as-fed-holds-rates-us-strikes-iran.html)
- A Reuters photo showed a notepad in front of Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent at a Camp David cabinet meeting reading "To Do Buy Japanese Yen $5-10 bil", photographed at 11:33 EDT on 31 July, "after Reuters earlier reported the Treasury had put banks on alert for a possible U.S. intervention in the market for Japan's currency." (Reuters caption as posted by @Acyn, 2026-08-01 01:02:08 UTC, https://x.com/Acyn/status/2083357579481366746)
- In January 2026 Bessent "dismissed speculation of US intervention in the currency market to sell dollars against the yen" and reaffirmed a "strong dollar policy", after a New York Fed rate check had fed speculation of a joint US-Japan intervention. (Trading Economics, "Yen Holds Decline as Bessent Dismisses Intervention", 2026-01-29, https://tradingeconomics.com/japan/currency/news/520912)
- On 18 March 2011 US monetary authorities intervened "buying $1 billion against Japanese yen" in a coordinated G-7 intervention carried out by the New York Fed. (Federal Reserve Bank of New York, "U.S. Monetary Authorities Intervened in FX Markets during the First Quarter", 2011-05-13, https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/news/markets/2011/fx110513)
