# Social media post on Taiwan and the PRC

> Friendly reminder that the People's Republic of China has never governed Taiwan for a single day. Since 1949 Taiwan has had its own government, its own military, and its own passports, and it has directly elected its presidents since 1996. Beijing's line that Taiwan has belonged to China since ancient times is pure invention: the island was never part of China until 1945.

# Fact sheet

- The Dutch held a colony in southwestern Taiwan from 1624 to 1662. Zheng Chenggong (Koxinga), a Ming loyalist, expelled them and his family's regime ruled parts of the island until 1683. (Encyclopaedia Britannica, Taiwan: History, https://www.britannica.com/place/Taiwan/History)
- The Qing dynasty took control of Taiwan in 1683 and administered it, first as part of Fujian province and from 1885 as a separate province, until 1895. Qing control over the mountainous interior and Indigenous areas was limited for much of that period. (Encyclopaedia Britannica, same URL)
- China ceded Taiwan to Japan under the 1895 Treaty of Shimonoseki. Japan ruled it as a colony until 1945, when the Republic of China (ROC) took over administration. (Encyclopaedia Britannica, same URL)
- The People's Republic of China (PRC) was proclaimed in October 1949, and the ROC government retreated to Taiwan. The PRC has never exercised control over Taiwan, which has been administered continuously by the ROC government since. (US Department of State, Office of the Historian, The Chinese Revolution of 1949, https://history.state.gov/milestones/1945-1952/chinese-rev)
- The ROC government in Taiwan maintains its own armed forces and issues its own passports. Martial law ended in 1987, and the first direct presidential election was held in 1996. (Encyclopaedia Britannica, same URL)
- UN General Assembly Resolution 2758 (1971) recognized the PRC's representatives as "the only lawful representatives of China to the United Nations" and expelled the representatives of Chiang Kai-shek. It does not mention Taiwan by name. (UN Digital Library, A/RES/2758(XXVI), https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/192054)
- As of 2026 the ROC (Taiwan) has formal diplomatic relations with 12 states. (ROC Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Diplomatic Allies, https://en.mofa.gov.tw/AlliesIndex.aspx?n=1294&sms=1007)
- PRC government position: "Taiwan has belonged to China since ancient times," "Taiwan is part of China," and Resolution 2758 embodies the one-China principle. (State Council Information Office white paper, The Taiwan Question and China's Reunification in the New Era, August 2022, http://english.www.gov.cn/archive/whitepaper/202208/10/content_WS62f34f46c6d02e533532f0ac.html)
