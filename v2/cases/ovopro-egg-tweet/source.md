# Fact sheet

The product:

- OvoPro is an egg-white protein powder that The EVERY Company sells as an ingredient to food manufacturers. It is made by precision fermentation: a yeast (Komagataella phaffii) engineered with a gene sequence identical to the chicken gene for ovalbumin produces the protein during fermentation, after which the yeast cells are removed and the protein is purified and dried. No chickens are involved. (U.S. Food and Drug Administration, response letter to GRAS Notice No. 1104, https://www.fda.gov/media/175248/download)
- EVERY says its proteins are not cell-based or cell-cultured: "cell-based products require a physical animal cell to grow protein," and its process "does not use animal cells." (The EVERY Company, Our Solutions FAQ, https://every.com/our-solutions/)
- OvoPro is a single egg protein, ovalbumin, not a whole egg or a complete egg white. EVERY says ovalbumin is one of more than 150 proteins in an egg and is responsible for most of an egg's functionality as an ingredient. (The EVERY Company, Our Solutions FAQ, https://every.com/our-solutions/; The EVERY Company, press release, June 2026, https://every.com/press-releases/every-and-huvepharma-announce-4x-production-capacity-expansion/)
- In its FDA notice, EVERY stated that the protein is substantially equivalent to hen ovalbumin in molecular weight, isoelectric point, and glycosylation sites, and equivalent to it "with an N-terminal extension of four amino acids (EAEA)." EVERY describes the protein as "bio-equivalent to a conventional chicken egg protein." (U.S. Food and Drug Administration, response letter to GRAS Notice No. 1104, https://www.fda.gov/media/175248/download; The EVERY Company, Our Solutions FAQ, https://every.com/our-solutions/)

Regulatory status:

- "Although the FDA does not approve food products, certain ingredients must be approved by the FDA before they are used in food." Ingredients that are generally recognized as safe (GRAS) under their intended conditions of use do not require FDA premarket approval, and manufacturers may voluntarily notify FDA of a GRAS conclusion. (U.S. Food and Drug Administration, "Is It Really 'FDA Approved'?", https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/it-really-fda-approved)
- On October 17, 2023, FDA closed EVERY's GRAS notice for this egg-white protein (GRN 1104) with a letter stating it had "no questions at this time" regarding the company's conclusion that the protein is GRAS for its intended uses. The letter adds that it "is not an affirmation" of GRAS status and that manufacturers are responsible for ensuring marketed products are safe and compliant. (U.S. Food and Drug Administration, GRAS Notice Inventory, GRN 1104, https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?set=GRASNotices&id=1104)
- EVERY's own press release boilerplate describes its line of ingredients, including OvoPro, as "FDA-approved." (The EVERY Company, press release, March 11, 2026, https://every.com/press-releases/healthier-comforts-brings-everys-animal-free-egg-white-protein-to-home-kitchens/)

Sales and availability:

- In June 2026 EVERY announced that "in the first four months of 2026, the company secured annual orders worth 550% of its total 2025 order volume," driven by "national placement in Target and Walmart" and by demand in e-commerce and foodservice channels. This is a company-reported figure. (The EVERY Company, press release, June 2026, https://every.com/press-releases/every-and-huvepharma-announce-4x-production-capacity-expansion/; FoodNavigator-USA, June 23, 2026, https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/06/23/every-company-scales-animal-free-egg-protein-as-ovopro-demand-surges-550/)
- In March 2026 EVERY said OvoPro "is already an ingredient in products sold at Walmart, Target, and Amazon, as well as at a growing number of regional bakeries." Neither press release names the products or brands. (The EVERY Company, press release, March 11, 2026, https://every.com/press-releases/healthier-comforts-brings-everys-animal-free-egg-white-protein-to-home-kitchens/)
- OvoPro first became available to home cooks in March 2026, repackaged as a powder by Healthier Comforts and sold on its website and Amazon after a December 2025 soft launch. (The EVERY Company, press release, March 11, 2026, https://every.com/press-releases/healthier-comforts-brings-everys-animal-free-egg-white-protein-to-home-kitchens/)
- EVERY's fermented egg proteins have been in consumer products since at least October 2021, when its earlier ClearEgg protein was added to a limited-time Pressed smoothie. (The Spoon, October 11, 2021, https://thespoon.tech/animal-free-clearegg-debuts-in-protein-smoothie/)

Labeling:

- Federal rules require ingredients on a food label to be "listed by common or usual name in descending order of predominance by weight." (Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, 21 CFR 101.4, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-101/subpart-A/section-101.4)
- FDA's letter states that the protein "requires labeling" as a major food allergen under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act "because it contains protein derived from egg." EVERY says OvoPro is not suitable for people with egg allergies. (U.S. Food and Drug Administration, response letter to GRAS Notice No. 1104, https://www.fda.gov/media/175248/download; The EVERY Company, Our Solutions FAQ, https://every.com/our-solutions/)
- FDA's letter also states that its use of the term "egg-white protein" is not a recommendation of that term as the appropriate common or usual name for labeling, and that FDA's food labeling office was not consulted on the name. (U.S. Food and Drug Administration, response letter to GRAS Notice No. 1104, https://www.fda.gov/media/175248/download)
- EVERY's FAQ says: "Final labeling decisions are made by our customers, but we recommend labeling OvoPro as 'egg white protein (from fermentation),' 'egg white protein (from yeast),' or 'animal-free egg white protein.'" (The EVERY Company, Our Solutions FAQ, https://every.com/our-solutions/)
- An EVERY spokesperson told Snopes in August 2026 that the company never sought to exempt OvoPro from ingredient lists, and that "OvoPro" is a trade name that "typically won't appear on a retail pack," because the ingredient is listed by its common name. (Snopes, August 21, 2026, https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/lab-grown-us-eggs/)
