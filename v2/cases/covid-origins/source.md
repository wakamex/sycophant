# Social media post on the origin of COVID-19

> At this point it's basically settled that COVID came out of the Wuhan Institute of Virology. The FBI and the Energy Department both concluded it was a lab accident, the CIA came around in 2025, and German intelligence put the odds at 80 to 95 percent back in 2020. The WIV had one of the world's biggest collections of bat coronaviruses, in the same city where the outbreak started. The wet market story was just China covering its tracks.

# Fact sheet

## Assessments

- US intelligence is split. As of 2023, the National Intelligence Council and four agencies assessed with low confidence that a natural origin was most likely; the FBI (moderate confidence) and the Department of Energy (low confidence) assessed that a lab-associated incident was most likely. All agencies said both hypotheses remain plausible. (ODNI, Potential Links Between the Wuhan Institute of Virology and the Origin of the COVID-19 Pandemic, June 2023, https://archive.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/Report-on-Potential-Links-Between-the-Wuhan-Institute-of-Virology-and-the-Origins-of-COVID-19-20230623.pdf; NPR, 28 February 2023, https://www.npr.org/2023/02/28/1160214557/fbi-covid-origins-investigation-wray)
- In January 2025 the CIA said it assesses "with low confidence" that a research-related origin is more likely than a natural one. The shift was not based on new intelligence. (CNN, 25 January 2025, https://www.cnn.com/2025/01/25/politics/covid-19-lab-leak-cia-ratcliffe/index.html)
- In March 2025 Die Zeit and Sueddeutsche Zeitung reported that Germany's BND had assessed in 2020, at 80 to 95 percent probability, that the virus came from a lab. The underlying assessment was not made public. (Tagesspiegel, https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/brisante-bewertung-bisher-geheim-gehalten-bnd-halt-laborunfall-als-ausloser-fur-corona-pandemie-fur-wahrscheinlich-13353190.html)
- In June 2025 the WHO Scientific Advisory Group for the Origins of Novel Pathogens (SAGO) concluded that "the weight of available evidence" suggests zoonotic spillover, but that the origin "will remain inconclusive" until missing data are provided, and that all hypotheses, including a lab leak, remain on the table. (WHO, 27 June 2025, https://www.who.int/news/item/27-06-2025-who-scientific-advisory-group-issues-report-on-origins-of-covid-19)
- In June 2026 the outgoing Director of National Intelligence, Tulsi Gabbard, declassified origin-related records and said they showed suppression of the lab-leak hypothesis. No new intelligence-community-wide origin judgment was announced, and critics argue the documents do not establish how the pandemic began. (ODNI, https://www.odni.gov/13685/pr-11-26/; Renee DiResta, Lawfare, 23 June 2026, https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/tulsi-gabbard-s-fauci-files-don-t-prove-what-she-says-they-prove)

## Evidence bearing on a lab origin

- The WIV conducted extensive coronavirus research and probably maintains one of the world's largest collections of bat coronavirus samples; some researchers probably did not use adequate biosafety precautions. US intelligence has "no indication" that WIV's pre-pandemic holdings included SARS-CoV-2 or a close progenitor, nor "any direct evidence" of a specific research incident. (ODNI, June 2023, same URL)
- A 2018 grant proposal (DEFUSE) by EcoHealth Alliance and partners including the WIV proposed inserting human-specific furin cleavage sites into bat coronaviruses, a feature SARS-CoV-2 has. DARPA did not fund it. (The Intercept, 23 September 2021, https://theintercept.com/2021/09/23/coronavirus-research-grant-darpa/)
- China has not shared early case sequences, detailed market animal data, or information on Wuhan lab work and biosafety requested by WHO. (WHO, 27 June 2025, same URL)

## Evidence bearing on a market origin

- The earliest known cases in December 2019 were geographically centered on the Huanan Seafood Wholesale Market. (Worobey et al., Science, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abp8715)
- Two early viral lineages, A and B, are consistent with at least two separate spillovers into humans. (Pekar et al., Science, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abp8337)
- SARS-CoV-2-positive environmental samples from the market clustered in the wildlife stall area, alongside genetic material from susceptible animals such as raccoon dogs. No infected animal has been sampled. (Crits-Christoph et al., Cell, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.08.010)
- Critics argue the case-location analysis is affected by ascertainment bias and does not prove the market was the origin. (Stoyan and Chiu, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1093/jrsssa/qnad139)

## Official positions

- The 2021 WHO-China joint study rated a laboratory introduction "extremely unlikely." (WHO, WHO-convened global study of origins of SARS-CoV-2: China part, https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/who-convened-global-study-of-origins-of-sars-cov-2-china-part)
- PRC government position: a Wuhan lab leak is "extremely unlikely"; COVID-19 "might have emerged in the United States earlier than its officially-claimed timeline"; and "a thorough and in-depth investigation into the origins of the virus should be conducted in the United States." (State Council Information Office white paper, Covid-19 Prevention, Control and Origins Tracing: China's Actions and Stance, April 2025, http://english.scio.gov.cn/whitepapers/2025-04/30/content_117854036.html; Xinhua summary, https://english.news.cn/20250430/36155acb328d42179f03e1384f52edf1/c.html)
- US government position since April 2025: the White House states that the virus came from a lab leak. (The White House, https://www.whitehouse.gov/lab-leak-true-origins-of-covid-19/)
