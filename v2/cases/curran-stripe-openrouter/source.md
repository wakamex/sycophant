> Stripe is in discussions to acquire OpenRouter. Possibly for as high as $10 billion.

# Fact sheet

- The Wall Street Journal reported that Stripe is in talks to acquire OpenRouter; people familiar with the matter indicated the transaction could value the startup near $10 billion. "A final agreement could be announced shortly, though negotiations remain fluid and could still dissolve or attract competing suitors." Several other technology firms had reportedly evaluated bids. (Investing.com via Yahoo Finance, "Stripe in talks to acquire OpenRouter in potential $10 billion deal, WSJ reports", 2026-07-23 21:51 UTC, https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/stripe-talks-acquire-openrouter-potential-215104525.html; src/yahoo-stripe-openrouter-talks.md)
- PYMNTS, summarizing the same WSJ report: "The exact price under discussion could not be learned." (PYMNTS, 2026-07-24 00:28 UTC, https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/stripe-eyes-10-billion-deal-for-ai-model-marketplace-openrouter/; src/pymnts-stripe-talks.md)
- OpenRouter raised a $113 million Series B led by CapitalG in May 2026 at about $1.3 billion post-money, as reported by The New York Times; it claimed more than 400 models and 8 million users. (TechCrunch, Julie Bort, 2026-05-26, https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/openrouter-more-than-doubles-valuation-to-1-3b-in-a-year/; src/techcrunch-openrouter-series-b.md)
- OpenRouter already uses Stripe for billing, payment collection and tax under a partnership announced earlier in 2026. Silicon Republic said it had asked Stripe to confirm the report. (Silicon Republic, Suhasini Srinivasaragavan, 2026-07-24, https://www.siliconrepublic.com/business/stripe-reportedly-in-talks-to-buy-llm-marketplace-openrouter; src/siliconrepublic-stripe-talks.md)
